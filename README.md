# Happy Birthday Arulselvi (chellowww) 💕

A responsive, romantic, and interactive web experience crafted to celebrate Arulselvi's birthday.

## ✨ Features & Interactive Stages

1. **Passcode Screen (Lock Screen)**
   - 3D-styled metallic pink heart padlock with a golden key and confetti ribbons.
   - 4-digit PIN input with interactive keypad (1-9, 0, Backspace).
   - **Passcode**: `2519`
   - Unlocks with crystalline audio chimes, celebratory confetti, and begins background music playback.

2. **Birthday Greeting & Excitement Prompt**
   - Header: *"Happy Birthday, Arulselvi"*
   - Animated vector art of cute teddy bears wearing party hats celebrating around a birthday cake with bunting banners.
   - *"Are you excited for what's next?"* dialogue with an inviting *"Yes ❤️"* button.

3. **Balloon Popping Game**
   - 4 pastel watercolor balloons (Sky Blue, Blush Rose, Mint Green, Lavender).
   - Responsive layout: 4-in-a-row on laptop screens, 2x2 grid on mobile phones.
   - Interactive popping physics with sound effects and confetti splashes.
   - Counter badge tracks progress (`0/4 POPPED` up to `4/4 POPPED`).
   - Reveals the secret message: **"You are so special"**.

4. **Birthday Cake & Candle Blowing**
   - Frosted chocolate/sponge cake with sprinkles and a glowing, flickering candle flame.
   - **Real Microphone Blowing Detection**: Tap *"🎙️ Blow into the mic"* and blow toward your device's microphone to extinguish the flame using Web Audio API!
   - Alternative fallback: Tap the candle or button directly.
   - Realistic rising smoke puffs and romantic dimming with message *"Close your eyes & make a wish ✨"*.

5. **Rose Bouquet & Floating Love Notes**
   - Centerpiece of fresh red roses wrapped in craft paper with a silky red ribbon.
   - 6 clean, non-overlapping floating love badges staggered gracefully around the bouquet:
     - *"Forever yours 🤍"*
     - *"You make my world beautiful ✨"*
     - *"You are my sunshine ☀️"*
     - *"Happy Birthday Arulselvi 💕"*
     - *"I'm lucky to have you 🥺"*
     - *"You're my favorite person 💖"*
   - Tap any badge to send heart particle reactions.

6. **Sweet Moments (Polaroid Photo Deck)**
   - 3D stack of Polaroid cards featuring your 5 uploaded couple & personal photos!
   - **Touch & Mouse Swiping**: Drag and swipe cards left or right to discard them with physics velocity and reveal the next memory.

7. **A Message From My Heart (Envelope & Love Letter - Grand Finale)**
   - Blush ivory envelope with gold metallic foil borders and a crimson heart wax seal.
   - Opens smoothly when tapped (and auto-unfolds when entering the stage) with sound and confetti!
   - Contains your full personalized love letter for *chellowww* in comfortable, legible typography.
   - Bottom includes the **↺ Replay Experience** button.
